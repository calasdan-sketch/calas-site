02 What it does

Three jobs, one dashboard.

Find the right businesses, learn the truth about each, reach out like a person — not a spammer.

01

Finds the businesses

Searches your whole territory (Manitoba, Saskatchewan, NW Ontario to start) using open business listings and maps. No paid lead lists, no "dollar a lead" — Calas's own finder does it.

02

Reads their website

For each company it reads their own pages — what they do, whether they're hiring or expanding, how many locations — and gives each a fit score out of 100, with plain reasons for how well it fits what you sell.

03

Reaches out properly

Checks the email address actually works, then drafts a short first email using only facts it found, with the source shown. You approve it, and the follow-ups stop the moment someone answers.

04 Said plainly

What Lead Me is, and isn't.

Honest outreach at speed, not a spam machine.

It finds and researches businesses from what they publish about themselves — it does not dig into anyone's private life, and it never sends without your approval. It drafts good first emails; it does not promise anyone will reply. These are cold contacts — businesses that haven't heard from you yet — so every email is short, true and easy to say no to. A fit score is an estimate of how well a business matches what you sell, not a promise that they'll buy.

06 Questions

Questions people ask.

Where do the leads come from?

Open business listings and maps, plus each company's own public website. No purchased lists, no scraping of people's personal information — just businesses that have chosen to be findable.

Which plan is right for me?

If you'd like to send each email yourself, Starter (C$199/mo) finds and checks the businesses, explains why each one fits, and writes the emails — you send them from your own email with one click. If you'd rather it ran in the background, Lead Me (C$500/mo) sends for you on a gentle schedule and stops by itself when someone replies. If a few of you sell into the same territory, Team (C$800/mo) gives up to four people one shared list, so nobody emails the same company twice, and exports to your CRM. None of the plans has a setup fee, and all three come with the same guarantee.

Will this get me in trouble with anti-spam rules?

It's built around Canada's anti-spam law (CASL): an unsubscribe link in every email, a daily send cap, a suppression list, and your real mailing address on the footer. You approve each message before it sends.

Does it email people automatically?

Only on the plans that do, and only the emails you've approved. On Starter, nothing goes out until you press “Open in my email” and send it yourself. On Lead Me and Team, it sends the ones you've approved from your own address — a first email, a follow-up on day 3 and a last one on day 7 — and it stops the moment someone replies or unsubscribes, and pauses if emails start bouncing.

Can it do my industry, not just forklifts?

Yes. It ships with seven ready-made trades and a builder to add your own, set up with you for your exact product and buyer.

Your responsibilities, in plain words

For each business, Lead Me explains why it’s likely fine to email under Canada’s anti-spam law (CASL), keeps a record of where each address was found, and skips businesses whose websites say they don’t want unsolicited email. That’s helpful information, not legal advice. You’re the sender, so you’re responsible for what you send and who you send it to — and if you’re ever unsure about a business, it’s always fine to skip it or ask us.
