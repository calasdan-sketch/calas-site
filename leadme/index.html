Questions people ask

Where do the leads come from?

Open business listings and maps, plus each company's own public website. No purchased lists, no scraping of people's personal information — just businesses that have chosen to be findable.

Will this get me in trouble with anti-spam rules?

It's built around Canada's anti-spam law (CASL): an unsubscribe link in every email, a daily send cap, a suppression list, and your real mailing address on the footer. You approve each message before it sends.

Does it email people automatically?

No. It drafts; you approve. It will only send the ones you've reviewed and marked ready, and it stops a sequence the moment someone replies.

Can it do my industry, not just forklifts?

Yes. It ships with several trades and a builder to add your own, so it fits whatever you sell.
