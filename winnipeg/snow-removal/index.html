What you get

  • Crews that have shown proof of insurance before they're offered your sites.
  • Reference-checked contractors, so the first storm isn't a test.
  • One point of contact for the season, instead of a new number every time it snows.

What it doesn't do

Dispatch Me doesn't guarantee a crew will arrive within a set time, and it doesn't replace your own site-liability review. It matches and tracks; the crews do the work under their own contracts.
