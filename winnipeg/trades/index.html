What it does on a Winnipeg job

  • You describe the work out loud on site: materials, labour, anything unusual.
  • It prices the job from your rate card, not from a guess.
  • The customer accepts by typing their name and can pay the deposit by bank or card.

It's built for plumbers and electricians who quote from the truck and are tired of writing estimates at the kitchen table after supper.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't set your prices, check your permits, or decide whether a job is safe or code-compliant. Those stay with you. If you also run subcontractors, the subcontractor insurance check tracks their certificates separately.
