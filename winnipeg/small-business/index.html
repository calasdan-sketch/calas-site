Two free tools for the paperwork and the scams.

Turn a folder of receipt photos into one spreadsheet, and check a suspicious text or invoice before you act on it. Both run in your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

Free. Built by Calas Automations, Winnipeg.

ReceiptSort

Drop in a folder of receipt photos and PDFs and get one CSV. It works on any phone or computer. Rows it isn't sure about are marked needs review, so you check those instead of all of them.

ScamCatch

Paste a suspicious text or screenshot and ScamCatch tells you in seconds whether it looks like a scam. It's a quick first check, not a guarantee, so when money or account access is involved, confirm with the sender through a number you already trust.
