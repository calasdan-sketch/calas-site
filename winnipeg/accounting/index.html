What it handles

  • Per-client lists of outstanding documents, so you can see who is holding up a file.
  • Reminders that go out on a schedule, so nobody on staff sends the follow-up.

What it doesn't do

On File doesn't prepare returns, give tax advice, or decide what a client's documents mean. It tracks and chases; your team does the accounting.
