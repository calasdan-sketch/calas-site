How it works

Set it up once. Check every load in seconds after that.

STEP 01

Enter your real costs, once.

Fuel at $/L and L/100 km, truck payment, insurance, maintenance, plates, labour — whatever it actually costs to run your truck for a month. Kilometres and CAD by default; miles if you want them.

STEP 02

It works out your cost per kilometre.

The one number every load should be measured against — not the rate a broker quotes you, the rate it actually costs YOU to move.

STEP 03

Type in a load's rate, loaded km and deadhead.

Haul Me instantly gives a green, yellow or red call, shows where the money goes (fuel, driver, fixed share, deadhead) and the floor rate per loaded km you can counter down to. Drag the fuel slider to see what a price jump does.

STEP 04

Every load, tracked by broker and lane.

A running log with totals by broker and by lane (origin → destination) and a one-click CSV export, so you can see who actually makes you money over time, not just this one trip.

Why cost per mile, not just the rate on the board

The same $2.20/mile load is a win for one truck and a loss for another.

The American Transportation Research Institute's 2026 Analysis of the Operational Costs of Trucking (released July 15, 2026, covering 2025 data) reports a total operating cost of US$2.336 per mile — but that's a US industry average. Your own truck's real number, once you count everything, is what actually decides whether a load is worth taking.

The honest trade-off. Haul Me only knows what you tell it. Feed it your real numbers — including the slow months — or the floor rate it gives you back will be wrong in the same direction your gut was.

What it is not

It's a profit brain for one job: is this load worth taking.

What Haul Me does

  • Works out your real cost per km (or mile) from the costs you actually have.
  • Gives a green / yellow / red call on any load's rate and distance, instantly, with the floor rate.
  • Flags deadhead before it quietly eats the trip's profit, and shows what it cost.
  • Totals profit and margin by broker and by lane; exports the log to CSV.

What it is not

  • Not a full TMS. No dispatch board, no ELD, no IFTA filing, no invoicing.
  • No onboarding project. Enter your costs, start checking loads the same day.
  • Not an accounting system. It hands you the numbers; your bookkeeper still books them.
  • Not a broker or load board. It doesn't find you loads — it judges the ones you already have.
