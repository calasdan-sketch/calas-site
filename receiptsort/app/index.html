Receipts in. One spreadsheet out.

Pick receipt photos or PDFs. ReceiptSort reads each one right here on your phone or computer and lists the date, vendor, subtotal, GST and total. Nothing is uploaded. Anything it isn't sure about is left blank and marked for you to check.

Lay the receipt flat in good light, fill the picture, hold steady.
