01 How it works

Four steps, one site visit.

No typing, no form, no app for the customer to install.

1

Talk it in.

Ninety seconds on your phone, standing in the customer's basement: what's wrong, what you'll do, what parts it takes. No typing, no form.

2

It's priced from your rate card.

Your labour rate, your markups, your usual parts — not an industry average. You see the quote first and change anything before it goes out. You must tap Reviewed before any quote can be sent — the AI never sends a price you haven't looked at.

3

The customer gets one link.

One link, by email today (text message is on the way). They read the quote and type their name to accept it. No app to install, no account, no PDF to open.

4

Deposit in, then you leave.

They pay the deposit from their bank account or a card, right there on their phone. You get told the moment it's done. The truck leaves with the job booked.

02 Why bank debit

A few dollars, not a percentage.

A deposit from the bank account costs you a few dollars. The same deposit on a card costs a percentage. Quote Me offers the customer bank debit first — a pre-authorized debit straight from their chequing account — and a card as the fallback. Here is why, using Stripe's published Canadian rates.

Pre-Authorized Debit: 1% + CA$0.40, capped at CA$5.00 per transaction, plus CA$5.00 on a failed or disputed debit. Stripe changes its pricing, and your own Stripe account may be on different terms. The dollar figures above are arithmetic from those two rates, nothing more.

Deposits go straight to the contractor's own Stripe account. Calas Automations never holds client funds.

The honest trade-off

A card clears in seconds. A bank debit is authorized on the spot but the money confirms over the following business days, and like any pre-authorized debit it can be disputed by the account holder. You decide which to offer, and the customer decides which to use. Quote Me just puts the cheaper one first.

03 What it is not

It quotes and it collects. That's the whole job.

What Quote Me does

  • Turns a voice note into a priced, written quote from your rate card.
  • Takes photos of the parts on site and puts them on the supplier order, so the right part gets ordered — not a guess.
  • Sends the customer one link; records their acceptance by typed name.
  • Takes the deposit, bank debit or card, and tells you when it lands.
  • Keeps a copy of every quote and acceptance so you can find it later.

What it is not

  • Not a job-management platform. No dispatch board, no scheduling, no inventory, no timesheets.
  • No onboarding. You send us your rate card, we load it, you talk your first quote. If you already run Jobber or ServiceTitan and it's working, keep it.
  • Not an accounting system. It hands you the numbers; your bookkeeper still books them.
  • Not a judgement on the job. It prices what you said. If you said the wrong thing, fix it before you send.
