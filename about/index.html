About

Honest automation, built in Winnipeg.

Calas Automations — pronounced “CAL-us”, and sometimes written Callus Automations.

Calas Automations builds simple software that takes the repetitive busywork off a small business’s plate — chasing documents, sending quotes, finding leads, pricing loads, watching for scams — so the people running the business can spend their time on the work that actually pays.

Who we are

We’re a small, independent software company based in Winnipeg, Manitoba. We build the tools ourselves and we stand behind them ourselves — when you call, you reach a real person who can actually fix things, not a call-centre script. That closeness is the point: you deal directly with the people who build what you’re using.

How we work

  • Try before you pay. Every paid tool has a free trial, and several of our tools are free forever.
  • No lock-in. Month to month, 30 days’ notice to cancel, always.
  • Your data stays yours. We don’t sell it, and the free tools keep your files on your own machine.
  • Real guarantees, in writing. If a tool doesn’t do what we promised, you don’t pay — and we mean it on the page, with specific numbers.

Why “Calas”

It’s a short name that’s easy to say once you’ve heard it: CAL-us. Plenty of people type it as “Callus” or “Callous” Automations — that’s us too. However you spell it, you’re in the right place.
