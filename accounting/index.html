What it does, what it does not do, what it costs, and how to start.

Questions

Five things people ask.

Do I need an app?

On File runs in your web browser; you sign in at onfile.calasautomations.com. Your clients need nothing installed: the requests and reminders reach them as emails.

What if a client never sends the documents?

On File keeps showing it as missing, with who was asked and when. Reminders go out when you or we press the button; nothing chases on its own yet. The conversation with the client is still yours.

Who sees my data?

We act as your service provider for the receipts, statements and signed forms and contact details you put in: we keep them confidential, use them only to run On File for you, let only our staff and the providers who need it see them, and delete them when you close your account. On File is stored on our server in Toronto, Canada. Reminder emails are sent through Resend (United States) and Bird (Netherlands). Details are in our Privacy Policy.

What if it gets something wrong?

On File does not check whether a document is valid or enough. It tracks and chases. A person at your firm still reviews and accepts what comes in. Reminders go out only when someone presses the button.

How do I cancel?

Cancel any time and you are never billed again; your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. Nothing is charged during the two-week pilot. Call (431) 244-9026 or email office@calasautomations.com. The workflow stays yours if you leave.

How we handle your data

  • We act as your service provider: your clients' documents and contact details are used only to run On File for you.
  • Only our staff and the providers who need it to run the service can see them. We do not use your content to train AI models.
  • On File is stored on our server in Toronto, Canada. Reminder emails go through Resend (United States) and Bird (Netherlands).
  • When you close your account, we delete your data, unless the law requires us to keep it.
