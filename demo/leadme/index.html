How this works

Every lead gets a fit score out of 100 from your industry's signals, showing how well it fits what you sell. A good fit is 60 or more; a best fit starts at 85. The best fits sit at the top. The batch only emails leads you have approved, so one tap after a big find never mails thirty companies nobody reviewed. Every email carries your mailing address and an easy way to say no, and a reply pauses the follow-ups.
