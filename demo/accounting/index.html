          The receipt they get
          Two taps and they're done. That's why people actually use it, and why they don't mind being asked.
          1. 1

            They send it through a private link

            The same client portal your firm already uses — not a new link that looks like a scam. It stays open for the receipt they find three weeks later.

          2. 2

            The file goes straight into your own folders

            Right into that client's folder, in whatever you already use. It never sits on our computers.

          3. 3

            Two receipts, right away

            Your client gets a note back listing what came in. Your checklist turns green. If neither happens, it didn't arrive.

          4. 4

            Written down, and never quietly erased

            Every request, reply, bounce and upload is written to a log in the client's own folder with a real timestamp, the template that went out, and who approved it. At close, a one-page chase record goes in the working-paper file.

          5. 5

            Nothing ticks itself

            A line turns green only when a file is attached to that line, for that year. The preparer can reject a file — wrong period, illegible, duplicate — which un-ticks it and restarts the chase for that item alone. "File it" stays locked until every line is verified.

          6. 6

            A cut-off date, and a partner who hears about it

            Each client gets a documents-in cut-off (their deadline minus your turnaround). The steps are scheduled backwards from it. Anything still outstanding at cut-off lands on a partner exception report: file with what we have, extension letter, or stop chasing.

          7. 7

            The T183 comes last, not first

            The T183 / T183CORP is requested only once the preparer marks the return ready, and is stored with its tax year, signature date and signing method. Nothing is EFILEd until that record exists.

          8. 8

            Bounces and strays don't vanish

            A bounced or wrong address goes straight to Sarah's call list with the reason. A file that doesn't match its client and line lands in an "unsorted" folder with a same-day task for reception — never in someone else's file.

          What we don't do

          • We don't keep copies. Nothing is saved on our side.
          • We don't open the documents. We only check that a file showed up. Your preparer checks it's the right one.
          • Your files stay where they already are. The one-page data flow — every place a file can rest, where it's hosted, who holds credentials — comes with the proposal. Replies with attachments get swept into the client folder and purged from the mailbox on a schedule you set.
          • You can shut it off any time. The files are still yours.
          • Named in your paperwork. Your engagement letter and privacy notice name Calas Automations as a service provider under PIPEDA, and the CPA Manitoba confidentiality undertaking is signed before the build starts.
          Keeping your clients' information private is your job, and we build around your rules — not ours.
