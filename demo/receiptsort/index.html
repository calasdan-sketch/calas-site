In the real app this downloads as a file that opens in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or your accounting software. In this demo nothing downloads; this is what's inside it.

What it does, and doesn't. ReceiptSort lists the date, vendor, subtotal, GST and total for each receipt, and marks anything it isn't sure of. It won't categorize expenses or reconcile a bank statement: one spreadsheet, that's the job. Spot-check the rows before anything goes to the CRA. Receipts, stores and amounts here are made up.
