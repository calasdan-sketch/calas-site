Card data and prices come from the Pokémon TCG API, which republishes TCGplayer (US dollars) and Cardmarket (euros) prices. Everything is converted to Canadian dollars with the exchange rate shown at the top. Prices are indicative market figures for raw (ungraded) cards, are updated by the sources on their own schedule (the date is shown per card), and can be thin or out of date for very rare cards. They are not offers to buy or sell. Dropwatch is not affiliated with Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, TCGplayer or Cardmarket.
