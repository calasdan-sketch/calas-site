Card data and prices come from the Pokémon TCG API, which republishes TCGplayer (US dollars) and Cardmarket (euros) prices. Prices are indicative market figures for raw (ungraded) cards, are updated by the sources on their own schedule (the date is shown per card), and can be thin or out of date for very rare cards.
