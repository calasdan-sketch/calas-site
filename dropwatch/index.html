The honest part

why you can trust the number

Nothing is guaranteed

Reselling is speculative — reprints, fees and dips are real, and some buys lose money. We show odds and the loss case, never promises.

No bots, ever

Manual buying within posted limits only. Purchase bots break retailer rules and we won't build or use them.

Real data or "unknown"

Prices come from feeds and APIs that allow it. We never scrape sites that forbid it, and every number carries its source, date and comp count.

Your data stays yours

Built free and local. Your watchlist and portfolio live in your own browser — no accounts, no cloud harvesting.
